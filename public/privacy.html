Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Athena is a private chief of staff app at athena.dragonflywebdesigns.com. Stephanie Holbrook built it and runs it for her own business, Dragonfly Web Designs. Only people she invites can sign in. This page explains what Athena collects, how it uses that information, and what you can do about it.

What Athena collects

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google, Athena receives your name, email address and profile picture. It uses them to sign you in and to check that your account is on the invite list.
  • Your calendar, if you link it. You can paste your calendar's private address into Settings so Athena can see your schedule and plan your day around it. You can also connect Google Calendar so Athena can add events when you ask for them (more on that below).
  • Your work in the app. Tasks, briefs, notes, content drafts, goals, and anything you type to Athena in chat.
  • Services you choose to connect. If you connect Granola, Athena copies your call notes and transcripts so it can turn them into to-dos. If you connect WonderReel or upload photos, Athena keeps the files you pick.
  • Public social media numbers. Athena collects public numbers, such as views and likes, for the social accounts you track.

Google user data

Athena asks Google for two things:

  • Sign-in (your name, email address and profile picture), so you can log in.
  • Google Calendar events (the calendar.events permission), used only to add an event to your calendar when you ask Athena to in chat. Athena never changes or deletes your events, and it does not read your calendar through this permission.

Information Athena receives from Google is used only for those two purposes. It is not sold, not shared with advertisers, not used for ads, and not used to develop, improve or train AI models. No person reads it, except when you ask for help with a problem or the law requires it.

Athena's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How Athena uses your information

  • To show your tasks, schedule and briefs in the app.
  • To write your daily brief, weekly plan and Friday wrap, and to answer you in chat.
  • To turn your call notes into to-dos when you have connected Granola.
  • To add calendar events, only when you ask.

Athena drafts. It never sends email, posts to social media, or publishes anything on your behalf.

Who helps run Athena

Athena relies on a few service providers. Each one handles only what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase stores Athena's database and handles sign-in.
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • GitHub runs Athena's scheduled jobs and keeps a private backup copy of your notes and tasks.
  • Anthropic provides Claude, the AI that writes your briefs and answers you in chat. It sees only what a given job needs.
  • Granola, WonderReel, Apify and Blotato, only if you connect or use them, for your calls, media files, public social numbers and post images.

Athena does not sell your information and does not share it with anyone else.

How your information is protected

Athena's database only lets the account owner read or change its records. Connection keys for outside services are kept on the server, and the app never shows them again after you save them. All traffic to and from Athena is encrypted.

How long Athena keeps it

Your information stays as long as your account is active. When you delete a task, note or file, it is removed from the app. To have everything deleted, email the address below and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Calendar, Granola or WonderReel at any time in Athena's Settings.
  • Remove Athena's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion by email.

Children

Athena is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
